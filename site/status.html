Status

The whole game plays in Azahar on both the New 3DS and Old 3DS models. It has run on a real console once, and the fixes from that run haven’t been tried on one yet.

v0.1.2Updated 18 October 2026
17 / 17story chapters, endings and Born From a Wish pass the automated playthrough
570 / 570doors and warps load in an automated run
0frames over 33 ms after a room load, on either model in Azahar
Part by part
PartStateNotes
Rooms, camera, lightingWorkingAll 17 areas, the PC camera’s roads, each room’s light record from the exe
FogWorkingThe fog layer with its wall test and vent puffs, and the EE’s fog fixes
ShadowsWorkingFlashlight shadow volumes and drop shadows, cast by enemies as on the PC
LoadingWorkingDoor changes in 50 to 90 ms in Azahar; the town streams tile by tile
Story, puzzles, endingsWorkingEvery stage program a player can reach, item combining, all endings, clear data and New Game+. Some rooms’ ambience and room code are still missing
Difficulty levelsTestingNormal passed the walk on foot before 0.1.2, the other levels all but one spot in the hotel. The walking test broke in 0.1.2 and needs fixing before it can say more
Enemies and bossesWorkingEvery enemy and boss, with holds. Not yet: Mannequins dropping from ceilings, creepers climbing onto them, confused enemies fighting each other
WeaponsWorkingAll eight, with the PC’s moves and pickups. The chainsaw and Hyper Spray turn up on a New Game after the endings that unlock them
MariaWorkingFollows and fights as on the PC, and plays Born From a Wish. Not yet: looking around, pointing, friendly fire, lying down in the resting rooms
Lake boatWorkingNew in 0.1.2, rowed with the PC’s controls from the town pier to the hotel
ControlsWorkingThe PC’s movement, quick turn, strafing and target lock; both layouts, remappable
Touch screen and menusWorkingLive map, items with models, memos, ten save files, the PC’s title, pause, options and game over
Scenes and moviesWorkingAll 128 cutscenes with face morphs, voices and subtitles, and every FMV. Movies have no subtitles yet and don’t follow the PC’s skip rules
Music and soundMostlyADX music and positional effects as on the PC. The music doesn’t change yet for low health or a nearby enemy, and menus and events don’t duck the sound
EffectsNot yetWater, specular highlights, blur and bloom, rain and smoke
Enhanced EditionWorkingIts fixes are built in and always on. The packer reads its packs, tested only on a synthetic copy
Original 3DSEmulator onlyLow quality, a 30 fps target and 64 MB, tested in Azahar’s Old 3DS model. Not yet run on a real one
Real hardwareTesting0.1.1 ran once on the author’s console. That run found characters too dark, stuttering cutscene audio, left and right turning swapped and a flashlight button that worked before James had the light. 0.1.2 fixes all four, unconfirmed on hardware, and nothing has been timed on a console yet
Help

Tried it on a console?

That’s the most useful report right now, good or bad. Include your model, your Luma3DS version and where you were. For a slow spot, a perflog 1 line in the debug file records frame times.
